# EXP-892: 重生2005 — 我带着未来20年记忆醒来 (Ch.1) — Storyboard

## Character Visual Guide
- **朱江 (22岁)**: Based on reference photo but YOUNGER — thinner face, NO goatee/facial hair, short black hair slicked back, wearing plain white T-shirt and jeans (2005 college student). East Asian male, medium build, determined eyes.
- **老王**: Round face, black thick-rimmed glasses, slightly chubby, messy hair, wearing oversized band T-shirt. Clearly different face from 朱江.
- **李姐**: 35-year-old Chinese woman, curly permed hair, sharp eyes, wearing colorful blouse, small gold earrings. Zhongguancun market vendor look.

## Common Prompt Prefix
"Semi-realistic manga style illustration, vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, bright vivid colors, warm lighting. Characters never look at camera/viewer, eyes always directed at objects or other characters within the scene."

## Panel List (40 panels)

### Beat 1: 醒来 (P1-P5)
P1: Close-up of an old CRT monitor casting blue light in a dark dorm room. The screen shows a Windows XP desktop. A Nokia 6600 phone vibrates on the desk next to it. Night scene, 2005 college dorm.
P2: Medium shot from behind — a young Chinese man (22, thin, no facial hair, short black hair, white T-shirt) sits up suddenly in a narrow bunk bed, looking disoriented. Dorm room with posters on wall, old desk, CRT monitor glowing.
P3: Close-up of the young man's hand grabbing a paper desk calendar. The calendar shows: 2005年6月15日 星期三. His hand is trembling slightly.
P4: Extreme close-up of the young man's wide eyes reflecting the calendar date. Pupils dilated, sweat on forehead. Expression: shock mixed with disbelief.
P5: Wide shot of the cramped 4-person college dorm room. Bunk beds, cluttered desks, CRT monitors, a poster of Jay Chou on the wall. Early morning light starting to filter through curtains. The young man sits on his bed staring at his hands.

### Beat 2: 确认 (P6-P9)
P6: Medium shot — the young man rushes into a small shared bathroom, hands gripping the sink edges, staring down. Fluorescent tube light overhead, white tiles, old mirror.
P7: Over-shoulder shot looking at the mirror reflection — a young face, smooth chin (no facial hair), sharp jawline, thin build. The reflection shows a younger version of the man, looking stunned at himself. The mirror is slightly foggy.
P8: Wide shot of the dorm — another young man (round face, thick black glasses, chubby, messy hair, oversized T-shirt) sits at a desk playing CS 1.6 on a CRT monitor, wearing big headphones. He turns to look at his roommate with a confused expression.
P9: Two-shot — the chubby roommate (老王) with headphones around neck looks up at the protagonist standing in the bathroom doorway. 老王 has a half-eaten instant noodle cup on his desk. His expression is "what's wrong with you?"

### Beat 3: 第一个念头 (P10-P14)
P10: Close-up of the protagonist sitting on his bed, hand on chin, eyes looking down-right in deep thought. A thought visualization: faded ghostly images of smartphones, Bitcoin logos, social media icons float around him like a dream sequence.
P11: Medium shot — the protagonist at the old desk, hands on the CRT keyboard, typing furiously on an Internet Explorer browser window. The URL bar shows "www.万网.com" (domain registrar). His expression is intense, calculating.
P12: Close-up of his face from the side, illuminated by the CRT's glow. A subtle smirk forming. Eyes sharp and focused. The blue light creates dramatic shadows.
P13: Split panel composition — left side shows 2005 (Nokia phone, RMB coins on desk, old browser), right side shows a ghostly 2025 vision (sleek smartphone, stock charts going up, WeChat logo). Visual representation of his two-timeline mind.
P14: Close-up of a crumpled bank passbook being opened. The balance shown: ¥487.32. The protagonist's fingers hold it tightly.

### Beat 4: 487元的窘境 (P15-P18)
P15: Medium shot — the protagonist hunched over the desk with a paper notebook, writing furiously with a ballpoint pen. The notebook shows a list of domain names with prices. His back is tense, concentrated.
P16: Over-shoulder shot looking at the notebook page. Visible handwritten text: domain names with prices (60元 each) and a running total. Some crossed out, some circled. A calculator (physical, old style) beside the notebook.
P17: The protagonist leaning back in his chair, looking up at the ceiling of the dorm room with a complex expression — frustration mixed with determination. One hand holds the bank passbook, other hand rests on the desk. The CRT monitor glows behind him.
P18: Close-up of his fist clenching on the desk next to the bank passbook showing ¥487. His knuckles white. A pen lies next to the notebook with circled domain names.

### Beat 5: 中关村之行 (P19-P24)
P19: Wide establishing shot — exterior of Zhongguancun Electronics City in 2005. Multi-story electronics mall building, huge Chinese signage, crowds of people, parked bicycles, old buses. Bright sunny day. The protagonist walks toward the entrance, small among the crowd.
P20: Interior of Zhongguancun — bustling narrow corridors lined with tiny electronic shops, CRT monitors on display, stacks of CD-ROMs, vendors calling out. The protagonist walks through, looking around. Neon signs, price tags everywhere.
P21: Medium shot — the protagonist approaches a small shop counter. Behind the counter sits a 35-year-old Chinese woman with curly permed hair, colorful blouse, gold earrings, sharp intelligent eyes (李姐). She looks up from a newspaper, sizing him up.
P22: Two-shot across the counter — 李姐 leans forward with interest, one hand on chin, looking at the protagonist who is explaining something with hand gestures. The shop background has computer parts, cables, and a whiteboard with prices.
P23: Close-up of 李姐's face — her eyes narrowed thoughtfully, a knowing smile forming. She's studying the protagonist with the look of someone who recognizes something special.
P24: Wide shot of the shop — 李姐 stands up from behind the counter, gesturing for the protagonist to follow her. The protagonist follows, looking cautiously optimistic. Other vendors peek from neighboring shops.

### Beat 6: 第一桶金的方向 (P25-P28)
P25: Two-shot in a small back office — 李姐 sits across a tiny table from the protagonist, pouring tea from a thermos into paper cups. Stacks of business cards and invoices on the table. She's talking animatedly.
P26: Close-up of the protagonist's face — he's suppressing a laugh, one corner of his mouth twitching up. His eyes show amusement. He's hearing about "making websites" as if it's cutting-edge technology.
P27: Medium shot — the protagonist sits at an old computer (running Windows 2000), hands on keyboard, staring at a Dreamweaver interface with Table-based layout. His expression is pained — a master forced to use primitive tools. The screen shows nested HTML tables.
P28: Over-shoulder shot of the protagonist typing PHP code on a text editor (Notepad++). The code is visible: basic PHP with mysql_connect. His posture shows resigned acceptance.

### Beat 7: 意外冲突 (P29-P32)
P29: Dorm room evening — 老王 sits on his bed, excitedly showing a printed letter/email to the protagonist. The letter has a Baidu logo visible. 老王's face is beaming with joy.
P30: Two-shot — 老王 gesticulating excitedly, and the protagonist sitting on the opposite bed, expression complex — knowing, slightly pained, but forcing a supportive smile. The gap between the bunk beds frames them.
P31: Close-up of the protagonist's eyes — looking at the Baidu offer letter his roommate holds. His eyes show inner conflict — he KNOWS Baidu stock will go 100x. But he also knows his path is different.
P32: Medium shot — the protagonist reaches over and claps 老王 on the shoulder firmly. His expression is warm but resolute. 老王 looks up at him, slightly surprised by the intensity.

### Beat 8: 第一个客户 (P33-P36)
P33: Wide shot — a small construction materials office. A middle-aged businessman in a polo shirt sits across a desk from the protagonist, looking at a laptop screen showing a website wireframe. The office has samples of tiles and building materials on shelves.
P34: Close-up of the protagonist's hands on the keyboard, typing HTML table layout code. The monitor shows a basic but clean business website taking shape. His fingers move with practiced speed despite the outdated tools.
P35: Medium shot — the businessman hands the protagonist a stack of cash (RMB bills, red 100-yuan notes). The protagonist receives it with both hands, a genuine warm smile on his face. The amount: 2000元.
P36: Close-up of the protagonist walking out of the office building into sunset light, holding the cash in one hand. He looks at the sky with an expression of quiet triumph — eyes slightly moist, genuine emotion. The warm orange sunset light hits his face.

### Beat 9: 夜晚的笔记本 (P37-P39)
P37: Night scene — the dorm room, dark except for a small desk lamp. 老王 is asleep in his bunk, one arm hanging off the bed. The protagonist sits at his desk, hunched over a physical paper notebook, writing with a pen. Intense concentration.
P38: Extreme close-up of the notebook page under lamplight. Visible handwritten timeline: "2007 — iPhone" "2009 — Bitcoin" "2011 — 微信" "2013 — 4G" "2015 — 大众创业" etc. The handwriting grows more frantic toward the bottom. His hand with the pen is visible, slightly trembling.
P39: Medium shot from the side — the protagonist has stopped writing, pen still in hand. He stares at the notebook page, face illuminated by the warm desk lamp light. His expression is a mix of excitement and the overwhelming weight of knowing the future. Shadows play across his young face.

### Beat 10: 悬念结尾 (P40)
P40: Dramatic close-up — the protagonist holds his Nokia phone to his ear, eyes wide with intensity. The phone's small screen glows green in the dark dorm room. His other hand grips the edge of the desk. Behind him, the notebook with the future timeline is visible under the desk lamp. His expression: alert, calculating, heart racing. The moment of decision.
