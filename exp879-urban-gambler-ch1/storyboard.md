## Character Visual Guide
- **朱江**: Chinese man, 30s, 172cm/80kg, stubble beard, black short hair combed back, big eyes. Dark gray suit with striped tie initially → white dress shirt only during poker → loosened tie at end.
- **陈铭远**: Chinese man, 50s, salt-and-pepper hair slicked back, thin/tall, hawk nose. Dark three-piece suit. Refined but sinister.
