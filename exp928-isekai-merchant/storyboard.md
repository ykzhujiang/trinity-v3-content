# EXP-928 异界商神·系统崛起 Ch1「降临」— Storyboard

## Character Visual Specs
- **朱江**: 172cm, 80kg, stocky build. Black short hair slicked back. Stubble/short beard. Big confident eyes. 
  - Modern: dark suit, white shirt
  - 异世界: worn brown linen merchant robe with rope belt
- **铜花**: 40yo woman, round face, messy bun hair, dirty white apron over brown dress, kind eyes, plump
- **赵掌柜**: Tall thin man, hawk nose, goatee, silk brocade robe in dark purple, calculating eyes
- **System Panel**: Semi-transparent blue holographic HUD floating in air
